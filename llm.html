In English

Mermoz Californie is an independent brand identity studio based in Paris, France. It works on strategy, visual identity, campaigns and design for brands.

Its view: every brand already carries its own story, convictions and singularity, but often looks at them from too close. The studio widens the field of view, starting with analysis to identify what is solid, then using intuition to open the angle and reveal the full extent of what was already there.
